# PSLV-QL Rocket Assembly | CAD Modelling & Assembly Design

## Project Overview

This project presents a 3D CAD assembly of the **Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle – QL (PSLV-QL)**, India's operational launch vehicle developed by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). The assembly represents the major structural and functional components of the launch vehicle, organised to illustrate its overall configuration, multistage architecture, and four-booster arrangement.

The model is developed with reference to publicly available technical information and configuration details published by ISRO. It aims to provide an engineering-oriented visual representation of the PSLV-QL, from its payload fairing and upper stages to its first-stage core and four solid-propellant strap-on boosters.

**Official ISRO Reference:** [Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle (PSLV) — ISRO](https://www.isro.gov.in/PSLV_CON.html)

## Vehicle Configuration

The PSLV is a four-stage launch vehicle featuring alternating solid and liquid propulsion stages. The PSLV-QL configuration incorporates four S12 solid-propellant strap-on boosters to augment the thrust generated by the first-stage core during the initial phase of flight.

The assembly represents the following major components:

- **Payload Fairing (Heat Shield):** The protective nose enclosure that houses the payload and shields it from aerodynamic and thermal environments during atmospheric ascent.
- **PS4 — Fourth Stage:** The uppermost propulsion stage, equipped with two liquid engines responsible for the final orbital injection of the payload.
- **PS3 — Third Stage:** A solid-propellant propulsion stage that provides thrust following the second-stage burn.
- **PS2 — Second Stage:** A liquid-propellant stage powered by the Vikas engine.
- **PS1 — First Stage:** The central solid-propellant core stage, which provides substantial initial thrust during launch.
- **Four S12 Strap-On Boosters:** Solid-propellant boosters arranged symmetrically around the first-stage core to augment launch thrust.
- **Interstage and Structural Sections:** Connecting and supporting sections that represent the interfaces between stages and contribute to the vehicle's overall configuration.
- **Payload and Upper-Stage Interfaces:** Components representing the upper vehicle arrangement and the accommodation of the payload within the launch vehicle.

> **Note:** The component list describes the principal vehicle systems. The exact components represented in the CAD assembly depend on the individual parts modelled and included in this repository.

## Engineering Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to understand and represent the configuration of a multistage launch vehicle through 3D CAD modelling and assembly integration.

Key objectives include:

- Reconstructing the overall geometry and arrangement of the PSLV-QL using publicly available reference material.
- Understanding the structural arrangement of a four-stage launch vehicle.
- Representing the integration of four strap-on boosters with the central launch vehicle core.
- Organising individual CAD parts into a coherent full-vehicle assembly.
- Studying the relationship between vehicle geometry, stage arrangement, and overall launch-vehicle architecture.
- Developing practical skills in aerospace CAD modelling, component integration, and technical visualisation.

## Technical Background

The PSLV is a versatile Indian launch vehicle used for missions involving Earth-observation, navigation, and scientific spacecraft. Its multistage architecture enables the vehicle to progressively discard spent stages and use the remaining propulsion stages to accelerate and inject the payload into its intended orbit.

The four PSLV configurations differ primarily in their strap-on booster arrangements:

| Variant | Strap-On Boosters |
| :--- | :---: |
| PSLV-CA | 0 |
| PSLV-DL | 2 |
| **PSLV-QL** | **4** |
| PSLV-XL | 6 |

The PSLV-QL provides a useful configuration for studying the integration of multiple external boosters with a central multistage launch vehicle.

**Source:** [ISRO — PSLV Configuration and Technical Specifications](https://www.isro.gov.in/PSLV_CON.html)

## Project Scope and Limitations

This repository is intended as an educational and portfolio-oriented CAD representation of the PSLV-QL configuration.

The model is based on publicly available reference information and should not be interpreted as an official ISRO engineering model, manufacturing drawing, or flight-qualified design.

Unless separately documented and validated, the assembly does not establish structural integrity, propulsion performance, aerodynamic characteristics, mass properties, or flight readiness. Any dimensions or component details not explicitly verified against authoritative sources should be treated as modelling assumptions rather than official specifications.
